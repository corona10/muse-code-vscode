# Open Muse Code

- Click the **Muse** icon in the editor title bar, or
- Press **Cmd+Escape** (Mac) / **Ctrl+Escape** to focus the chat, or
- Press **Cmd+Shift+Escape** / **Ctrl+Shift+Escape** to open a new tab.

Choose where Muse lives with the `museCode.preferredLocation` setting: the side bar or an editor tab.
